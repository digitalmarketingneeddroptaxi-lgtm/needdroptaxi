Frequently Asked Questions about Outstation Rides What is an
                Outstation Ride and how do I book one? An outstation ride is a long-distance journey from your starting location to another city or town. We
                offer one-way, round-trip, and multi-stop options. You can easily book through our website or by calling
                customer support. Simply choose your pickup location, destination, and type of ride, and we'll handle
                the rest. How are outstation
                rides priced? Our outstation rides are priced based on the distance traveled. We have transparent pricing with rates
                starting at ₹14/km for sedans and ₹19/km for SUVs. Additional charges may apply for tolls,
                parking, or
                state taxes. These will be clearly communicated during booking. For one-way trips, you only pay for the
                distance traveled, with no return fare. Can I make multiple
                stops or change my route during the trip? Yes, we offer a multi-stop option where you can make planned stops along your route. Select this during
                booking. If you need to change your route or make unscheduled stops during the trip, inform the driver.
                Additional charges may apply based on extra distance or time. What types of
                vehicles are available and can I request a specific driver? We offer a variety of vehicles including hatchbacks, sedans, and SUVs. Choose the vehicle that best
                suits your travel group and luggage needs during booking. While we allocate drivers based on
                availability, you can request a specific driver if you've had a positive experience, and we'll try to
                accommodate your preference. Are there any
                minimum requirements or night charges? Our outstation rides typically require a minimum distance of 130 km. Night charges may apply for trips
                between 10 PM and 6 AM. These charges are clearly communicated during booking. For shorter trips, our
                local rental service may be more suitable. How soon can I book
                and what if I need to cancel? We recommend booking at least 12 hours in advance, but we can accommodate urgent requests when possible.
                If you need to cancel, notify us as soon as possible. Cancellation policies may apply, and you'll be
                informed of any charges at the time of booking. What about tolls,
                taxes, and breaks during the trip? Tolls, parking fees, and state taxes (if applicable) are to be paid separately by the passenger. These
                costs are typically calculated at the end of the journey. Your driver will stop for necessary breaks
                during long trips, and you can request additional breaks as needed. How experienced are
                your outstation drivers? All our drivers are experienced with long-distance driving and are well-versed in popular outstation
                routes. They ensure you have a safe and comfortable journey, no matter the distance.
