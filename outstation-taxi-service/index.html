Frequently Asked Questions about Outstation Rides

An outstation ride is a long-distance journey from your starting location to another city or town. We offer one-way, round-trip, and multi-stop options. You can easily book through our website or by calling customer support. Simply choose your pickup location, destination, and type of ride, and we'll handle the rest.
Our outstation rides are priced based on the distance traveled. We have transparent pricing with rates starting at ₹14/km for sedans and ₹19/km for SUVs. Additional charges may apply for tolls, parking, or state taxes. These will be clearly communicated during booking. For one-way trips, you only pay for the distance traveled, with no return fare.
Yes, we offer a multi-stop option where you can make planned stops along your route. Select this during booking. If you need to change your route or make unscheduled stops during the trip, inform the driver. Additional charges may apply based on extra distance or time.
We offer a variety of vehicles including hatchbacks, sedans, and SUVs. Choose the vehicle that best suits your travel group and luggage needs during booking. While we allocate drivers based on availability, you can request a specific driver if you've had a positive experience, and we'll try to accommodate your preference.
Our outstation rides typically require a minimum distance of 130 km. Night charges may apply for trips between 10 PM and 6 AM. These charges are clearly communicated during booking. For shorter trips, our local rental service may be more suitable.
We recommend booking at least 12 hours in advance, but we can accommodate urgent requests when possible. If you need to cancel, notify us as soon as possible. Cancellation policies may apply, and you'll be informed of any charges at the time of booking.
Tolls, parking fees, and state taxes (if applicable) are to be paid separately by the passenger. These costs are typically calculated at the end of the journey. Your driver will stop for necessary breaks during long trips, and you can request additional breaks as needed.
All our drivers are experienced with long-distance driving and are well-versed in popular outstation routes. They ensure you have a safe and comfortable journey, no matter the distance.

Travel Tips

  • Plan your itinerary in advance to make the most of your outstation trip.
  • Carry essential documents like ID proof.
  • Inform the driver about any specific routes or stops you prefer.
  • Keep some cash handy for toll fees and other incidental expenses.
  • Download offline maps of your destination for navigation in areas with poor network coverage.
