Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total travel time from Madurai to Rameshwaram?

The journey typically takes around 3-3.5 hours, depending on traffic and road conditions. We ensure timely breaks if needed for your comfort.

Are there any additional charges besides the quoted fare?

No, our fares are all-inclusive covering fuel, toll charges, driver allowance, and GST. There are no hidden charges.

Can I book for an early morning pickup?

Yes, we offer 24/7 pickup service. Early morning pickups are available as per your convenience.

What happens if the car breaks down?

We maintain our vehicles regularly, but in rare cases of breakdown, we provide immediate replacement vehicles to ensure your journey continues without major delays.

Do you provide pickup from Madurai airport?

Yes, we provide pickup services from Madurai airport and any other location within Madurai city limits.
