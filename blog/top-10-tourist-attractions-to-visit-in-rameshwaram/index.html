Top 10 Tourist Attractions to Visit in Rameshwaram with Need Drop Taxi

Rameshwaram, a serene island in Tamil Nadu, is steeped in history, mythology, and natural beauty. Renowned as one of the Char Dham pilgrimage sites, it�s a place where spirituality meets scenic landscapes. Whether you�re visiting for religious purposes or exploring its rich heritage, Need Drop Taxi is your reliable travel partner to navigate this enchanting destination.

1. Ramanathaswamy Temple

Renowned for its intricate corridors stretching over 1,200 meters, which are among the longest in the world, and its 22 sacred theerthams (water tanks), the Ramanathaswamy Temple stands as a testament to both spirituality and architectural brilliance. Each theertham is believed to possess unique holy properties, attracting pilgrims from far and wide to perform sacred ablutions. Dedicated to Lord Shiva, the temple�s majestic Dravidian architecture, adorned with towering gopurams and intricate carvings, invites awe and reverence, making it a must-visit for both devotees and admirers of cultural heritage.

Things to Do: Read the inscriptions on the walls to take a closer look at the historical importance of the temple

Timings: Monday- Sunday from 4:30 am�1 pm & from 3 pm�8:30 pm

Recommended Duration: 1 hour

Entry Fee: Rs.50

2. Pamban Bridge

A marvel of engineering, the Pamban Bridge is India�s first sea bridge, connecting Rameshwaram to mainland India. This cantilever marvel offers a thrilling experience with panoramic views of the Bay of Bengal. As you cross, witness the rhythmic motion of trains and vehicles harmonizing with the surrounding waves---a true testament to innovation and scenic beauty.

Things to Do: Drive through the bylane of this 2km sketch of the bridge and let the fresh breeze touch your face.

Timings: Open all-day

Recommended Duration: 5 Minutes

3. Dhanushkodi Beach

Dhanushkodi, often referred to as the �Ghost Town,� is a mesmerizing destination steeped in history, tragedy, and natural splendor. Once a bustling town, it was decimated by a catastrophic cyclone in 1964, leaving behind haunting ruins that narrate tales of resilience and loss. The remnants of the old church, railway station, and houses stand as silent witnesses to the fury of nature and the passage of time. The serene beaches with crystal-clear waters provide a stark contrast to the eerie ruins, creating an atmosphere of tranquility and reflection. Experience the enigmatic charm and historical depth of Dhanush Kodi with Need Drop Taxi, your trusted travel partner to this evocative destination.

Things to do: One can spend time at the rather deserted and clean beaches here.

Timings: Open all-day

Recommended Duration: 45 Minutes

4. Agni Theertham

Located near the Ramanathaswamy Temple, Agni Theertham is one of the 22 sacred theerthams associated with the temple and holds immense spiritual significance. Pilgrims flock to this beach to take a holy dip, which is believed to cleanse them of sins and grant spiritual purification. The serene waves lapping against the shore offer a tranquil setting for reflection and devotion. Besides its religious importance, the spot provides mesmerizing views of the ocean, making it an ideal place for both spiritual seekers and nature enthusiasts.

Things to do: Take a dip at Agni Theertham first, if you need to take a dip inside the Ramanathaswamy Temple. Hence this is the first stop during your One Day Madurai to Rameshwaram Trip.

Timings: Monday- Sunday, from 5 am to 6 pm

Recommended Duration: 30 Minutes

Entry Fee: Free

5. Kothandaramaswamy Temple

Nestled amidst serene surroundings, the Kothandaramaswamy Temple is steeped in mythological significance. It is believed to be the sacred spot where Lord Rama met Vibhishana, symbolizing acceptance and righteousness. Surrounded by calm waters and lush greenery, the temple�s tranquil atmosphere invites visitors to reflect and connect with its spiritual heritage. Its unique setting, amidst the scenic beauty of Rameshwaram, makes it a captivating site for pilgrims and history enthusiasts alike.

Timings: Monday: Sunday from 6 am to 7 pm

Recommended Duration: 20 Minutes

Entry Fee: Free

6. Adam�s Bridge (Ram Setu)

Delve into the fascinating history of Adam�s Bridge, also known as Ram Setu, a chain of limestone shoals believed to have been constructed by Lord Rama and his vanara (monkey) army to reach Lanka. This mythical marvel, steeped in Ramayana lore, symbolizes faith, devotion, and ancient engineering prowess. A boat ride across the azure waters offers a closer look at this enigmatic natural formation, sparking curiosity and awe among visitors. Witness the breathtaking views and immerse yourself in the tales of yore, making your trip to this iconic site truly unforgettable.

7. Ariyaman Beach

Perfect for family outings, this beach offers calm waters, picnic spots, and water sports. Need Drop Taxi ensures a smooth journey to this delightful destination.

Things to do: One can spend time at the rather deserted and clean beaches here.

Timings: Open all-day

Recommended Duration: 45 Minutes

8. Gandhamadhana Parvatham

This sacred hillock offers panoramic views of Rameshwaram. It�s believed to house Lord Rama�s footprints and is an important site for pilgrims.

9. Jatayu Tirtham

Dedicated to Jatayu, the bird from the Ramayana, this tirtham is a serene spot for spiritual reflection.

Timings: Monday: Sunday from 6 am to 7 pm

Recommended Duration: 20 Minutes

Entry Fee: Free

10. Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Memorial

Pay homage to India�s �Missile Man� at this well-maintained memorial, which showcases his life, achievements, and contributions.

Things to Do: Apart from looking back at the achievements of the great scientist, one can shop for Rameswaram souvenirs or even seashell items here.

Timings: Monday- Friday from 8 am to 7 pm

Closed On: Weekends

Recommended Duration: 30 Minutes

Entry Fee: Free
